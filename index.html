A session is three moves.

The same three every time, which is why they're on the mark.

01

Tap

Hold your phone to the tag on the machine. Crosses opens on that machine and claims it for you, even from the lock screen.

02

String

A timer runs while you work. Extend it if the job is bigger than you thought. The room sees the machine as in use the whole time.

03

Log

Finishing frees the machine and writes the job to the room's history: racquet, string, tension, who did it.

02

Stuck to the machine

A tag costs about a dollar.

crossesapp.com/m/4f2a9c

One tag per machine.

Pair a blank NFC sticker to a machine once, from inside the app. After that the tag is the shortest path into a session: no menus, no picking the right machine off a list.

With Crosses installed, the tap opens the app straight to that machine and starts the session.
Without it, the same tag opens a web page naming the machine, so a guest stringer or an Android phone still gets somewhere useful.
